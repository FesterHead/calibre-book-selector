Calibre Book Selector Plugin
Version: 1.0.1
Author: FesterHead
License: MIT

Intelligently select eligible books and series entries to add to your Reading List queue with automatic order progression.

Features:
- Single Target Reading List ('Next')
- Automatic exclusion of read and in-progress books (% Read > 0)
- Strict series progression ensuring books are queued in order
- Author and series separation spacing constraints
- Automatic list order increment tracking
- Interactive dialog with live search and 1-click quick random picker
